About

Born at the table, among friends for whom wine was always the main topic.

Founded in 2024, BrodWine has one aim: to bring the wine scene of Slavonski Brod to life and make the finest Croatian and international wines accessible to members and wine lovers across Brod-Posavina County. Every gathering is curated – the winery, the wines, the venue and the company.
